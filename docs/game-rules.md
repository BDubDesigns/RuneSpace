# Game Rules (current design direction)

> **This document records stable current design direction. It distinguishes implemented/current systems, approved direction not yet implemented, and explicitly future/out-of-scope work.** For detailed contracts see the linked authoritative docs — this document does not duplicate their full mechanics or balance values.

## Platform

- RuneSpace is **browser-first** and **mobile-friendly**.
- It is a low-fi sci-fi RPG inspired by the progression, quests, social texture, and long-term grind of old-school MMORPGs and action-point games. It is **not** a RuneScape clone.

## Progression

- **Progression is central** to the experience.
- Active play may be **more efficient** than passive/offline play.
- Passive/offline systems, if any, must be **explicit and server-resolved**. The client never computes offline gains.
- **Botting is forbidden.** Any authorized automation must be an in-world system introduced later, not client-side scripting.
- Authoritative timing, action resolution, inventory, and XP contracts are in `docs/gameplay-foundations.md`.

## Implemented early-game systems

The following are live on `main` as server-authoritative, Play-orchestrated systems (see `docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, `docs/missions.md`, and `docs/location-scenes.md` for detailed contracts):

- **Play orchestration** — generic transaction/action lifecycle, shared state assembly, and client Play shell (`server/action-resolution.ts`, `server/play.ts`, `features/play/`).
- **Travel & Scavenging** — walking between locations, scavenging yields, and Power Annex / Power Cell claims.
- **Mining** — Ferrite Shale Mining at The Jag (Power Cell boosting, run history).
- **Refining** — authored recipes at the Abandoned Processing Yard, run a chosen number of batches at a time, or Max until blocked (bounded runs, #229).
- **Welding & Cargo Hold** — ship Cargo Hold, Landing Gear (#322) and Propulsion System (#330) repair at Crash Site, the Crew Stop repair in Holo Hollow, and Welding progression.
- **Oil Shale Mining (#342, #380)** — The South Cut (and, since #380, Stillreach's The Dry Seam on the identical shared source), reached over the travel-only South Access Track, is Skatboll's Mining-5 Oil Shale source with a low-yield outcome, a Sapphire / ultra-rare Motherlode Slab secondary table, and the per-character Prioritize primary resources setting (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Oil Shale at The South Cut").
- **Site stashes** — a permanent per-character stash mount at The Jag, Rusk Recovery, the Abandoned Processing Yard and Deep Jag (#284), built through the ordinary Welding repair flow and gated only by personal Welding level, with any owned container installed to supply slots (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`).
- **Practice Welding & Clean Pass** — the repeatable Welding training loop at Wade's Workbench in Rusk Recovery, run a chosen number of welds at a time or Max until the Scrap runs out, and the two optional Clean Pass opportunities every Welding work unit rolls (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`).
- **Holo Hollow Landing and Last Leg Trading (#357)** — Skatboll's walkable landing pad at axial (1,1), joined to the Crash Site, the Abandoned Processing Yard and the South Access Track, with Toll Kade's shop as its one Local Place: three replayable Talk topics on foot, and a fuel counter that the server opens only for a character who has completed and reported Thrust Issues (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Completed-Mission merchant unlock and Last Leg Trading"). No ship state, flight or Stillreach.
- **The Rivet Utility Shuttle and its first flight (#358)** — one persistent ship per character with a berth and a 300 FU tank, loaded from Inventory or the attached Cargo Hold with Full Small Fuel Canisters; the single Ship panel at its berth; Wade's Move Along (started by the Thrust Issues report, completed by the flight's own landing, no reward); a 15-second, 10 FU authoritative flight from the Crash Site to Holo Hollow Landing that lands lazily and offline-safely, moves the character, ship and Cargo Hold together, and leaves a durable dismissible arrival notice. Nothing flies back to the Crash Site; the Stillreach route came with #375 (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "The Rivet Utility Shuttle and its first flight").
- **Stillreach Slice A (#373)** — a second planet with its own planet-scoped local map of eight scenery-only World Locations (Stillreach Landing Station, Windward Track, Hilltop Workshop, Prospecting Quarry, The Low Hum, The Survey Line, The Dry Seam, Harmonic Workings) joined by seven authored walking edges at the ordinary 24-second leg (Werner's Lab, #429, became the ninth location with two more), with no activity, status plate, merchant or ship of its own. Reached in play since #375 by the Rivet (below); Admin → Teleport / Set Location also places a character on it. Skatboll's 11-location map is unchanged (`docs/travel-map-design.md`, "Planet-scoped maps")
- **Werner's Lab (#429)** — Stillreach's ninth World Location at axial (1,2): a parked mineral laboratory with two authored walking edges, to the Prospecting Quarry and Harmonic Workings, and none to The Survey Line despite the hex touching it. Werner stands at the location itself (no Local Place, Mission or unlock): three always-available replayable topics (About the Lab, The Quarry, Gem Cutting) and a Trade counter that sells Stone at 5 Credits (12 per character per Pacific day, selling back restores nothing) and buys Stone at 2 and Uncut Quartz, Topaz and Sapphire at 8, 18 and 35. It is also the ninth Stillreach Beginner Echo target; hunts already running keep their stored target (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Werner's Lab").
- **Stillreach Slice B (#375)** — a repeatable Rivet flight between Holo Hollow Landing and Stillreach Landing Station, two authored routes of 120 FU and 260 seconds each, from the ship's actual berth with the three repairs and a reported Thrust Issues, carrying the character, the ship and its Cargo Hold together; a nonblocking return-fuel warning; the full takeoff, interplanetary chart and touchdown cinematic in both directions over the approved 15:8 art; the ship in Stillreach's fungal ring on the approved 4:1 mark while berthed there; and Last Leg Trading's automated terminal at the Landing Station, the same merchant as Toll's counter with shared daily allowances (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "The interplanetary leg" and "Last Leg Trading's Stillreach terminal"). No Mission completes on these landings.
- **The Wise Old Enby at Hilltop Workshop (#389)** — Stillreach's resident researcher, present for any visitor with three replayable topics, who offers *Not From Around Here* in person to a visitor carrying a Rune Fragment or intact Runestone and completes it with their RuneSpace explanation, no reward; *About RuneSpace* is replayable afterwards (`docs/missions.md`, `docs/npc-conversations.md`).
- **Runeworking and the First Runeworking Lesson (#391)** — Runeworking, the fifth skill (Electric Rose accent), at the Enby's Resonance Rig at Hilltop Workshop, open once *Not From Around Here* is complete: one deterministic recipe, the Beginner Echo Seeker (1 Rune Fragment → 1 Seeker, 34 ticks, 25 XP, Runeworking 1), run by number or Max with Finish Current. *First Runeworking Lesson* follows the mystery by continuation or, for earlier completions, the Enby's offer: three Seekers attuned after acceptance, then the Enby's turn-in for 150 Runeworking XP. The Enby's Fragment advice depends on the character's Mining level. Seekers have no Use yet (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Runeworking at the Resonance Rig").
- **Rune Fragment Mining at Harmonic Workings (#383)** — Stillreach's Mining-8 Rune Fragment source (75 ticks, one or two Fragments and 130 XP on a success, a miss awards nothing), with one success-only 40-outcome find roll: an Intact Runestone 1/8 (silent, +130 XP when kept) or an Uncut Sapphire 1/20 (+30 XP, RARE FIND only when awarded) (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Rune Fragment Mining at Harmonic Workings").
- **Stone Mining at the Prospecting Quarry (#379)** — Stillreach's Mining-1 Stone source (33 ticks, 1–2 Stone, 45 XP, a miss awards nothing), with a 600-outcome exclusive secondary table (Rune Fragment 1/4, Uncut Quartz 1/6, Uncut Topaz 1/12, Ultra Rare Boulder 1/200), and a 2-Credit Stone buyback at the Stillreach Last Leg Trading terminal and, since #429, at Werner's Lab (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Stone at the Prospecting Quarry" and "Werner's Lab").
- **Fuel manufacturing (#343)** — the Empty Small Fuel Canister (Fabrication 8, 1 Galvanic Stock) and the Full Small Fuel Canister (Refining 8, 10 Oil Shale + 1 Empty canister, a failure returning 5 shale and the shell, 50 Fuel Units): two ordinary tradable stackable items on the existing engines. The Rivet loads them into its tank (#358; see "The Rivet Utility Shuttle" below) (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Fuel manufacturing").
- **Rune materials (#367)** — Rune Fragments (200 g, stack 10) are a Mining Secondary Find at The Jag (1 in 25), Deep Jag (1 in 8) and The South Cut (1 in 6), worth +20 Mining XP, and the intact Runestone (1,000 g, stack 5) breaks apart from Inventory Item Details into exactly five Fragments; Runestones have no natural source yet (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, "Rune materials").
- **FedHex, the Legendary miner's set and rarity tiers (#399)** — Beginner Echoes now roll a 0.2% (1/500) Legendary on each planet, Skatboll's Miner's Boots (2 kg) and Stillreach's Miner's Gloves (1 kg), with Rune Fragment at 30%: unique tradable equipment for the new real Gloves and Boots slots, each worth +1 effective Mining level for success chance only. A Legendary that does not fit is mailed to the FedHex mailbox, opened from two always-open pickup Local Places at the two landings (one shared interior), and claimed one parcel at a time; every Legendary discovery posts one LEGENDARY FIND line in General. Legendary tiles carry the sparkles plus a sweep and a label, Ultra Rare the sparkles only, and a trade holding either shows a nonblocking scarcity warning. See `docs/gameplay-foundations.md`.
- **Fabrication & Tinkering** — Fabrication at Rusk Recovery's Fabrication Station: the four Tier-1 recipes (#232), Fabrication 5's Direct Galvanic Scrap, Galvanic Wire Spool, two-Cell Power Cell batch, Loadsteel Cutter and Wheel Assembly (#322), and Fabrication 8's Drive Mount (#330), Freight Harness (#233) and Empty Small Fuel Canister (#343); a binding workpiece whose materials stay reserved until it resolves, numeric or Max runs, the optional Manual Override push-your-luck layer, and Tinkering — dismantling finished Fabrication output for Fabrication XP and Scrap Metal (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`).
- **Inventory & Equipment** — carried stacks/unique items, slot/mass capacity, containers (the starter MYKEA and the fabricated Scrap Box and Freight Harness), two Mining tools (the Salvage Cutter and the Mining 5 Loadsteel Cutter), and Equipment, all resolved through one authored equipment-definition boundary (#233).
- **Locations & presentation** — location scenes and the local world map.
- **Holo Hollow, Local Places & Credits** — the first settlement, one-level Local Places inside it, character-scoped Credits, and Bix's authored merchant Trade (`docs/gameplay-foundations.md`, `docs/holo-hollow.md`).
- **Missions & NPC conversations** — declarative mission framework (`docs/missions.md`), authored missions (Walk It Off / Cut Your Teeth / Waste Not / Hold It Together / Keep the Change / 10,000 Hours / 10,001 Hours / Return the Favor / Break It Down / Brace Yourself / Wheel Be Right Back / Thrust Issues / Move Along / A Cut Above / Not From Around Here / First Runeworking Lesson, plus the optional Out of the Weather, Cutting Costs and Curly Must-Stash), the one canonical NPC conversation model with replayable topics (`docs/npc-conversations.md`), and dialogue authoring (`docs/qc-studio.md`).

- **Lapidary (#431)** — Stone practice and Quartz cutting at Werner’s Lapidary Workbench, unlocked through the four sequential stages of They’re Minerals, Marie!; supervised one-piece lessons precede permanent quantity/Max access. Cut Topaz (Lapidary 5, 30 s, 150 XP) and Cut Sapphire (Lapidary 8, 45 s, 300 XP, #433) follow on level alone, with a Lapidary 8 "Beyond the Basics" Werner topic.

## World & skills

- The opening direction is a **one-way crash-site tutorial planet**.
- **Mining** is the first core skill direction; **Refining** is the second (at the Abandoned Processing Yard); **Welding** repairs the crashed ship's Cargo Hold at Crash Site, and is practised, as often as the player likes, at Wade's Workbench in Rusk Recovery; **Fabrication** turns processed stock into discrete items at the Fabrication Station beside that bench, taught by Tansy after 10,000 Hours; **Runeworking** attunes Rune Fragments at the Wise Old Enby's Resonance Rig on Stillreach.
- Planetary maps use **hexes** with **local fog-of-war** exploration.
- **Explore** consumes **limited fuel**. The Rivet's first flight (#358) is the first fuel spend: 10 Fuel Units for the Crash Site to Holo Hollow Landing hop.
- **Speeder Piloting** and **Ship Piloting** are separate skill directions.

## Content & validation

- Game content should be **data-driven** and **validated** (Zod schemas in `game/schemas/`, typed definitions in `game/content/`).
- Content is referenced by **stable IDs** (see `game/schemas/ids.ts`), never by inline literals in UI code.

## Non-goals (currently)

The following remain explicitly out of scope until later approved issues: additional quest/mission content beyond the current authored missions, additional crafting/gathering activities beyond the approved Mining/Refining/Welding/Fabrication/Runeworking slices (Resonance Echo hunts and any Seeker Use are future work) (Fabrication beyond its level-8 Freight Harness, landing-gear repair, site-stash electronics and remote manifests, original-material Tinkering recovery, and further tool tiers are future work), hex exploration, fuel consumption beyond the approved Cargo Hold repair, ships/speeders, combat, Phaser minigames, chat/clans/multiplayer, player-to-player trading and any player-driven market or economy simulation, a CMS, background workers, and autonomous issue selection. See `docs/gameplay-foundations.md` for the authoritative slice boundaries.

The approved NPC merchant loop (Credits and Bix's fixed buy/sell catalog, issue
#159) is live and described in `docs/gameplay-foundations.md` and
`docs/holo-hollow.md`. It is deliberately not a market: prices are authored
content, stock is fixed, and no merchant wallet, restock timer, dynamic pricing,
or player-to-player exchange exists.
